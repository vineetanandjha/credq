Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for first home buyer planning.

Can I buy with a smaller deposit?

Possibly. The answer depends on your lender, savings history, income and any scheme support that may apply.

Should I get pre-approval first?

In many cases, yes. Pre-approval can help you understand your budget and show sellers you are prepared.

What if I am not sure what I can borrow?

Use the borrowing power calculator or request a free assessment so we can review the numbers with you.
