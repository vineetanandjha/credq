When to refinance your home loan

The right time to refinance is when the numbers, your goals and lender conditions line up. A lower advertised rate alone is not enough.

Check My Timing

Signs it may be time to refinance

  • Your rate is no longer competitive compared with current offers.
  • Your fixed rate is ending and the revert rate is too high.
  • Your property value has increased and your equity position is stronger.
  • You want better features such as offset, redraw or split-loan flexibility.
  • Your income or expenses have changed and the loan needs to fit your cash flow.

When waiting may be smarter

Refinancing may not help if break costs are high, your credit file needs repair, your income evidence is temporarily weaker, or you plan to sell very soon. In those cases, a rate negotiation or short-term plan may be more suitable.

Review before a major change

It can be worth reviewing your home loan before parental leave, a new investment purchase, debt consolidation, renovations or a business change. Lender assessment can be easier before your income profile changes.
