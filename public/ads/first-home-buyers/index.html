Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for first home buyer planning.

What is a mortgage broker, and how can they help first home buyers?

A mortgage broker compares lender options, explains borrowing capacity and helps prepare the loan application so you can avoid common delays.

How much deposit do I need?

Some lenders and schemes may allow lower deposits, while a 20% deposit can help avoid LMI. The right path depends on your savings, income and property price.

Can I use a first home buyer grant?

Possibly. Eligibility depends on state rules, property type, purchase price, residency and whether you have owned property before.

Should I get pre-approval before making offers?

Pre-approval can help you understand your budget and act with more confidence, although it is still subject to lender conditions and property assessment.

Will Afterpay or credit cards affect my loan?

They can. Lenders assess commitments, account conduct and available credit limits, so it is worth reviewing these before applying.
