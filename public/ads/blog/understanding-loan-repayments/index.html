How Home Loan Repayments Work in Australia

Understanding the inner mechanics of your monthly home loan repayment helps you take control of your mortgage early. In Australia, home loan interest is calculated daily on the outstanding principal balance and charged to your account at the end of each monthly billing cycle.

1. Principal and Interest Repayment Mechanics

In the early years of a standard 30-year principal and interest mortgage, the majority of your monthly payment goes toward covering accrued interest charges, with a smaller portion paying down the actual debt principal. As the principal drops, the daily interest charge decreases, accelerating the rate at which your debt declines in later years.

2. The Fortnightly Repayment Strategy

Switching from monthly repayments to accelerated fortnightly repayments is one of the simplest ways to pay off your home loan faster. By taking your monthly repayment figure, dividing it by two, and paying that amount every two weeks, you make the equivalent of 13 monthly payments each year instead of 12.

  • Example ($600,000 Loan at 6.00% p.a.): Paying fortnightly shaves up to 4 years off a 30-year mortgage and saves over $90,000 in total interest.
