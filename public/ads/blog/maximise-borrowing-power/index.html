How to Increase Your Borrowing Power

Discover actionable steps to optimize your serviceability, eliminate hidden credit liabilities, present clean living expenses, and find the Australian lender that maximizes your property loan capacity.

Unlocking Your Maximum Mortgage Capacity

In a competitive Australian real estate market, your borrowing power directly dictates which suburbs and property types are within reach. Many first home buyers assume borrowing power is a fixed dollar number determined purely by gross annual salary. However, borrowing capacity is a dynamic calculation that varies by up to $100,000 or more between different Australian financial institutions for the exact same applicant profile.

At CREdQ, our credit specialists help first home buyers audit and refine their financial profile 3 to 6 months before applying. By taking targeted steps to improve serviceability and eliminate unnecessary assessment penalties, you can legally and safely maximize your property budget.

1. Reduce Liabilities & Cancel Unused Credit Facilities

The fastest and most direct way to boost home loan serviceability is reducing existing debt commitments. Australian responsible lending policies require banks to assume high repayment commitments for un-hedged consumer debts.

The Credit Card Limit Multiplier Effect

Lenders do not care whether your credit card balance is $0 at the end of every month. Banks must calculate your ongoing debt liability using 3% of the total approved credit limit as an ongoing monthly expense. A $15,000 credit limit creates an assumed monthly liability of $450, reducing borrowing capacity by ~$70,000.

  • Action: Cancel unused credit cards entirely, or reduce limits to $1,000–$2,000 before submitting your mortgage application. Ensure written confirmation of cancellation is obtained for underwriting verification.

Clear Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) & Personal Loans

Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) facilities like Afterpay, Zip, or Klarna are viewed by bank credit assessors as risk indicators of poor cash flow control. Consolidating or paying out personal loans, car loans, or novated leases substantially frees up monthly debt-servicing capacity.

2. Clean Up Account Conduct & Living Expenses

Underwriting algorithms evaluate bank statement account conduct over the 90 days preceding application lodgement.

3-Month Transaction Audit

Lenders scrutinize your primary transaction account statements for recurring discretionary outflows. To present an optimal living expense profile:

  • Eliminate Overdrafts & Overdrawn Fees: A single overdrawn fee or dishonored direct debit can trigger manual underwriting referral or automated credit scoring declines.
  • Reduce Food Delivery & Gambling Transactions: Frequent UberEats, DoorDash, or gambling transactions signal high discretionary spending. Reducing these 3 months prior to applying ensures your declared expenses closely match lower HEM benchmark baselines.
  • Streamline Recurring Subscriptions: Cancel redundant streaming, gym, or digital subscription memberships that inflate declared living costs.

3. Optimize Income & Understand Lender Shading Policies

Not all income dollars are treated equally by bank credit calculators. Presenting your earnings in alignment with favorable lender policies can dramatically increase borrowing results.

Maximizing Secondary & Variable Income

  • Overtime & Allowances: Select lenders accept 100% of essential services overtime (e.g., nurses, police, paramedics) while major banks shade standard corporate overtime to 80%. Matching your employment type to the right bank policy maximizes assessed income.
  • Second Jobs: Most banks require a 12 to 24-month continuous history in a secondary job before counting that income. However, specialist lenders will accept second job income after just 6 months.
  • Boarder / Rental Income: If purchasing a home with an attached granny flat or renting out spare rooms, select lenders allow boarder income to contribute to serviceability calculations.

4. Joint Applications & Structuring Considerations

Applying as a couple or joint applicant combines gross earnings, which substantially expands total borrowing capacity. However, joint applications also combine living expense baselines and debt liabilities.

Joint Application Tips

  • Pay Off Individual Debts First: Clear personal debts held by either partner before lodging a joint application.
  • Align Credit Scores: Check both applicants' Comprehensive Credit Reporting (CCR) scores via Equifax or Illion to resolve active default listings or incorrect address records.
  • Evaluate Single vs Joint Application: If one partner has high personal liabilities or non-concessional income, a single application structure may occasionally yield superior results depending on legal ownership advice.

5. Choosing Lenders with Favourable Assessment Models

Australian residential lending is not a one-size-fits-all market. Banks differ significantly in how they calculate serviceability:

  • LMI Capitalization Rules: Some lenders calculate LVR including LMI, while others exclude LMI from the primary LVR threshold, allowing higher borrowing net of fees.
  • HEM Tier Benchmark Discrepancies: Lenders utilize different versions of the Household Expenditure Measure index, resulting in baseline living cost variations of several hundred dollars per month.
  • HECS Assessment Formulas: Certain non-bank lenders assess HECS repayments strictly on actual ATO repayment rates, whereas older bank calculators apply conservative gross income percentage bands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do borrowing limits differ between Australian banks?

Banks utilize custom serviceability calculators with different rules regarding overtime shading, living expense benchmarks, self-employed income averaging, and assessment interest rate buffers.

How much does cancelling a credit card boost borrowing capacity?

Cancelling a $10,000 credit card limit typically increases maximum home loan borrowing capacity by approximately $45,000 to $50,000 because lenders assess credit card liabilities at 3% of the approved limit per month.

How many months of bank statements do lenders review?

Lenders review 3 consecutive months of primary transaction and credit card accounts to audit discretionary living costs, verify regular income deposits, and check account conduct.

Does clearing HECS/HELP debt increase borrowing power?

Yes. Clearing a HECS/HELP balance increases net monthly take-home pay, which directly improves serviceability and can boost borrowing capacity by tens of thousands of dollars.
