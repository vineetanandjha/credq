Pre-Approval Explained

Understand how conditional home loan pre-approval works, what documents you need, and how to shop for property with genuine confidence.

What Is Home Loan Pre-Approval?

Home loan pre-approval (also referred to as conditional approval or approval-in-principle) is an official indication from an Australian mortgage lender that they are willing to lend you a specified sum of money, subject to verifying a specific property valuation and final underwriting conditions.

Why Pre-Approval Is Essential Before Searching

  • Defines Your True Ceiling Price: Prevents you from wasting time viewing properties outside your verified borrowing budget.
  • Required for Auction Bidding: Bidding at auction is unconditional. Having written pre-approval ensures your lender is ready to inspect and approve the property valuation.
  • Signals Serious Intent to Real Estate Agents: Sellers and real estate agents prioritize buyers who present written pre-approval over unassessed buyers.

Documents Required for Pre-Approval

  1. Proof of Identity: Passport, Driver's Licence, Medicare Card.
  2. Income Verification: 2 recent payslips, PAYG Payment Summary, or 2 years of Tax Returns for self-employed applicants.
  3. Bank Statements: 3 months of consecutive primary savings and transaction account statements.
  4. Liabilities: Recent statements for credit cards, personal loans, or HECS debt balances.
