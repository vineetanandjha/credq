What the First Home Guarantee is

A clearer path to home ownership for eligible buyers who have saved a deposit but do not yet have a full 20%.

The First Home Guarantee is the scheme many Australians still search for when they are trying to buy with a smaller deposit. The scheme now sits under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, which means the terminology has changed, but the core idea is the same: help eligible buyers enter the market sooner.

For many first home buyers, the biggest barrier is not finding the right property. It is getting to the deposit and keeping settlement costs manageable. This is where the guarantee can make a meaningful difference, because it can reduce the amount of cash you need upfront and may remove the need to pay LMI.

CredQ can help you work through the practical side of the process, including lender policy differences, location-based price caps and how the guarantee fits with your borrowing power.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions buyers usually ask first.

Is the First Home Guarantee still available?

Yes, but it is now part of the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme. People still commonly use the older name, so both terms are worth searching.

Can I buy with a 5% deposit?

In some cases, yes. The scheme is designed to help eligible buyers purchase with a deposit as low as 5%, subject to scheme and lender criteria.

Do I need to pay lenders mortgage insurance?

One of the key benefits is that eligible buyers may avoid LMI, which can significantly reduce upfront purchase costs.

Are all properties eligible?

No. The property must be within the current location-based price caps and otherwise meet the scheme and lender rules.
