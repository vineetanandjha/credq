Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common refinance questions.

Can I refinance my home loan?

Yes, if your income, credit profile, property value and loan purpose meet lender requirements. CredQ can review whether a refinance is worthwhile before you apply.

Why refinance a home loan?

Borrowers commonly refinance to lower repayments, improve features, access equity, consolidate debt or restructure lending for a new life stage.

How does refinancing work?

Your new lender pays out your existing loan at settlement and your repayments move to the new loan. The process usually includes assessment, valuation, approval and discharge.

What are the costs to refinance?

Costs can include discharge fees, government registration charges, application fees, valuation fees and fixed-rate break costs. We compare these against likely savings.

When can you refinance a home loan?

You can review refinancing at any time, but it is especially useful after a rate rise, income change, property value increase, fixed-rate expiry or when your goals change.

How much equity do I need to refinance?

Many lenders prefer at least 20% equity to avoid lender mortgage insurance, but options vary by lender and scenario.
