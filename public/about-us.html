Vineet Anand - Principal Broker

After working in professional services and helping people with complex decisions, I wanted to create something that makes finance easier, clearer, and stress-free for everyday Australians.

At CredQ, we believe that accessing the right mortgage shouldn't be complicated. Our mission is to demystify the lending process and empower you to make informed decisions about your financial future.

With years of experience in the financial services industry, I've seen firsthand how the right guidance can transform someone's relationship with money. That's why CredQ exists – to be your trusted advisor, not just a service provider.
