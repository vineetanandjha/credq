Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for refinance planning.

When should I refinance?

It depends on your savings, fees, exit costs and how long you plan to keep the new loan.

Can refinancing reduce my repayments?

Often yes, but the right answer depends on the new rate, loan term and associated costs.

Can I release equity when refinancing?

In some cases, yes. It depends on the value of the property, your loan balance and lender policy.
