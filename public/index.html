Common questions

Quick answers to the questions buyers, refinancers and investors usually ask first.

How much can I borrow?

Use the calculator above for an indicative estimate. The final borrowing power depends on your debts, income, living costs and lender policy.

Do you help first home buyers?

Yes. The first-home page covers deposit support, scheme pathways and lender comparison for buyers getting into the market.

Can refinancing save me money?

It often can, but the right answer depends on the rate, fees, exit costs and how long you plan to keep the loan.

Do you work with low deposit scenarios?

Yes. We can review low-deposit options and check the lender requirements before you make a commitment.

How fast will I hear back?

We aim to respond quickly on business days so you are not waiting long for a next step.
