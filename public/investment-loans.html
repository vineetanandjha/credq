Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for investment lending.

Do investment loans use different lender rules?

Yes. Rental income, servicing policy and acceptable loan structures can all differ from owner-occupier lending.

Can I use equity from another property?

In some cases, yes. We can review whether equity release is possible and sensible for your plan.

Should I talk to a broker before I buy?

Usually, yes. A quick strategy review can save time and help you avoid the wrong loan structure.
