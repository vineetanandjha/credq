Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for low deposit planning.

Can I buy with less than 20% deposit?

Possibly, depending on lender policy, income, property type and whether any scheme support is available.

Will I pay LMI?

It can depend on your deposit size and the loan structure. We can review whether any support or lender option changes the outcome.

Should I speak to a broker first?

Yes, especially when the deposit is tight. A broker can help avoid lender options that are unlikely to fit.
